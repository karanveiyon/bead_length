Calculation Formula

Larger piece slope:
S₁ = (D₁max − D₁min) / L₁

Smaller piece slope:
S₂ = (D₂max − D₂min) / L₂

Average slope:
S = (S₁ + S₂) / 2

Diameter difference across missing section:
ΔD = D₁min − D₂max

Missing length:
ML = ΔD / S

Total reconstructed length:
Total = L₁ + ML + L₂
Important: This calculation assumes that the original glass tube had a approximately straight/linear taper. If the tube has a curved, irregular, or manufactured profile, the slope method will only provide an estimate.
